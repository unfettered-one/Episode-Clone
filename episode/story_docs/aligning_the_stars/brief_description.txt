When your hot, mysterious celebrity invites you to a charity gala, you are determined to be reunited. Will the stars align for you to find each other?
Your life takes an unexpected turn when you cross paths with Adrian Vega, a mysterious rising celebrity who seems to see something in you that no one else does. Just as you’re ready to forget the fleeting moment, a surprise invitation arrives: Adrian wants you at a glittering charity gala—an event filled with glamour, secrets, and watchful eyes.

The spotlight is dazzling, but dangerous. Will you dare to chase this spark with Adrian, or will the world of fame tear you apart before your story can begin?
